📖 Mission briefing

It is 2 a.m. on the night shift at the Teaching Hospital. A power cut has frozen the clinical pharmacy's medication-adherence audit, and the quality committee needs the report at 8 a.m. The survey of 20 patients is locked behind six digital locks. Fifteen forms were recovered as PatientSurvey_part1_P01-P15.xlsx; five are still on paper (cards P16–P20). Use SPSS to recover the numbers – and keep every patient anonymous.

Rules: 100 pts per lock · +20 first-try bonus · hint = −20 pts · +5 pts per minute left at the end · roles rotate every lock (Driver, Navigator, Checker, Recorder).
